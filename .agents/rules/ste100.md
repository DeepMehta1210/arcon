# Rule: ASD-STE100 Compliance (Simplified Technical English)

Follow these rules for all documentation, code comments, system instructions, and generated outreach text:

1. **Sentence Length:**
   - Procedural and instruction sentences must have a maximum of 20 words.
   - Descriptive sentences must have a maximum of 25 words.

2. **Grammar & Tone:**
   - Use the active voice. Do not use the passive voice.
   - Use the imperative mood for instructions and procedures.
   - Put one instruction or command in one sentence.
   - Write one topic per paragraph.

3. **Vocabulary:**
   - Do not use corporate jargon, buzzwords, or idioms.
   - Do not use sycophantic greetings (e.g. "I hope this email finds you well").
   - Eliminate all banned AI tokens and filler words.
   - Keep sentences clear, direct, and factual.
